Indexing into a vector of vectors is similar to indexing into vectors, we just have to be careful about whether we need to index into the **outer vector** or the **inner vector**. Let's see how this works: **Video Recap** Indexing into a vector of vectors _selects a vector_. To select an element from that vector, _index again_.
