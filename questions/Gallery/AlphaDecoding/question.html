A monoalphabetic cipher is an improvement on the Caesar cipher. Itreplaces each letter of the alphabet with another letter of thealphabet, based on a translation table. Only letters are converted,spaces and punctuation marks retain their value. The convertionretains case (i.e., lowercase letters are converted to lowercaseletters, uppercase letters are converted to uppercase letters).
