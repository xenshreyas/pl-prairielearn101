The Caesar cipher is a simple cipher that replaces each letter of thealphabet with another letter of the alphabet, found $k$ elementslater, where $k$ is the key for encryption. Only letters areconverted, spaces and punctuation marks retain their value. Theconvertion retains case (i.e., lowercase letters are converted tolowercase letters, uppercase letters are converted to uppercaseletters).
