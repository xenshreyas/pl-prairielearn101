Playtesting may focus on players' experience, behaviours that emerge, frustrations, or even trying to break the game by systematically putting it into "edge cases". This process may reveal new test cases and design insightsthat update the whole design. In CPSC 210, we often go through the software development process (design, testing, development) and afterwards do only brief manually testing.During this lab, it can be helpful (and fun!) to playtest your work alongside your unit tests as your implementation progresses to see your work in action.
