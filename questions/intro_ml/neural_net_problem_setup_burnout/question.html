You are hired by a large U.S. corporation to help them use machine learning to identify employees that are at risk of burnout due to Zoom fatigue. They want to create an application that will generate a message in the employee's calendar if they are at risk of Zoom fatigue in a particular workweek, like this: There will be one version of the calendar message for employees at risk of moderate Zoom fatigue, and another message for employees at risk of extreme Zoom fatigue. If the employee's risk for the week is low, there will be no message at all in the calendar. To train your model, they give you a spreadsheet of data drawn from employees' calendars and surveys of employees. The spreadsheet has the following columns * record index (an auto-incrementing unique ID for each row in the spreadsheet) * number of Zoom calls employee participated in that week * average duration of Zoom calls the employee participated in that week (in minutes) * average time between Zoom calls that week (in minutes) * employee experience (number of months this employee has worked at this corporation) * Zoom fatigue level that week (0 for not much Zoom fatigue, 1 for moderate Zoom fatigue, 2 for extreme Zoom fatigue) based on survey at the end of the week. (Note: this survey was used to collect training data, but when your model is used "in production", employees will not be surveyed about Zoom fatigue!) You decide to use a fully connected neural network with a single hidden layer to solve this problem. You will train the network using the features from the spreadsheet that are relevant for predicting Zoom fatigue. * * * How many input units will your network have? (Not including any bias unit.) * * * How many output units will your network have? * * * How many units will you use in the hidden layer? (Not including any bias unit.) * * * What activation function will you use at the hidden units? relu sigmoid tanh softmax linear * * * What activation function will you use at the output units? relu sigmoid tanh softmax linear
